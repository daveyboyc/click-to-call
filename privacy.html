Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 April 2026

Click to Call ("the App") consists of a Chrome browser extension and an Android application that work together to let you send phone numbers from web pages to your Android device for dialing. This policy explains what data is collected and how it is used.

What data we collect

The App collects and processes only the minimum data needed to function:

Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token — a unique identifier assigned to your Android device by Google's Firebase service. This token is used to route phone numbers from the browser extension to the correct device. It is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal information.

Phone numbers — when you click a detected phone number in your browser, it is sent through the relay service to your paired Android device. Phone numbers are transmitted in transit only and are not stored on any server.

How we use the data

The FCM device token is used solely to deliver phone numbers from the browser extension to your paired Android device via Firebase Cloud Messaging. Phone numbers pass through the relay as transient data and are not logged or retained on the server.

The extension stores your settings (relay URL, device token, default country, and recent send history) locally in Chrome's sync storage on your own device.

Third-party services

The App uses Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications from the relay to your Android device. Firebase is operated by Google and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. No other third-party services are used.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties. The device token is sent only to Google's Firebase service for the purpose of delivering messages to your device.

Data security

All communication between the browser extension, the relay service, and Firebase uses HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit. The relay service enforces rate limiting to prevent abuse.

Data retention and deletion

No personal data is stored on the relay server. To remove all locally stored data:

Chrome extension: uninstall the extension or clear its data from chrome://extensions.

Android app: uninstall the app or clear its data from Android Settings. This invalidates the FCM device token.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
