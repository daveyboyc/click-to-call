# Chrome Web Store listing draft

## Name
Click to Call

## Summary
Send phone numbers from Chrome to your Android phone and open them in the dialer instantly.

## Description
Click to Call detects phone numbers on web pages and lets you send them to your paired Android phone with one click.

Ideal for CRM workflows, directory websites, property sites, spreadsheets, and any page containing phone numbers.

### Features
- Detects phone numbers directly on web pages
- Converts supported numbers to a clean dialing format
- One-click send to a paired Android device
- QR-based pairing flow between browser and phone
- Recent send history in the extension popup
- Simple on/off toggle

### How it works
1. Install the Android app and open it
2. Pair your phone with the extension
3. Open any webpage with phone numbers
4. Click a detected number
5. Your Android phone receives it and opens the dialer flow

### Privacy
Click to Call sends only the phone number you click and your paired device token to the configured relay service so the request can reach your Android phone.

The extension does not collect browsing history or sell personal data.

Productivity

## Single purpose justification
This extension detects phone numbers on pages and sends a clicked number to the user's paired Android phone for dialing.
