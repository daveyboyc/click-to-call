# Google Play listing draft

## App name
Click to Call

## Short description
Send phone numbers from your browser to your Android phone for instant dialing.

## Full description
Click to Call pairs your browser with your Android phone so you can move phone numbers from the web to your dialer in one tap.

Use it when browsing contact pages, CRMs, spreadsheets, listings, directories, or any site that contains phone numbers.

### What it does
- Pairs with the Click to Call browser extension
- Receives clicked phone numbers from your browser
- Shows a prompt on your Android device
- Opens the dialer with the number ready to call
- Supports a quick QR-based pairing flow

### Typical setup
1. Install the Android app
2. Install the Click to Call browser extension
3. Pair the phone and browser
4. Click a phone number in your browser
5. Confirm on your Android phone and open the dialer

### Good for
- sales workflows
- recruitment
- customer support
- property and directory sites
- admin work from a desktop browser

### Notes
The app works with the companion browser extension and a relay service used to deliver the clicked number to your device.
